# Competitive notes — Lug²Lug (lug2lug.org)

**As-of:** 2026-09-13  
**Access:** Public pages + signed-in Apple session (taste quiz skipped). No Market+ purchase.

## What they are

Catalog-first enthusiast platform (~15k watches): specs (incl. lug-to-lug), collection/wear tracking, favorites/wishlist, compare, owner-thread synthesis, forum-oriented asking-price index. **Market+** paid add-on (~$39.99/yr or ~$4.99/mo): live asks, closing asks, trends, listing alerts.

## Presentation strengths

- Hero photography + floating key-spec badges  
- Grouped spec chapters  
- Market as its own chapter (Retail vs asking; tabs eBay / Shops / Market+)  
- Owners Talk with sources  
- Pill product nav; airy editorial layout  

## Presentation costs

Long pages, many CTAs, paywall mid-decision, motion/hover density.

## Signed-in inventory (observed)

My Watches (box / wishlist / favorites / collections), compare (≤4, true-scale overlay), profile/social light features, settings (currency, theme, motion).

## Vitrine response

| Stance | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Match** | Museum detail, key specs, calm market chapter, free core / paid depth pricing rhyme |
| **Surpass** | Personal rubric/deal/design scores; confidence/insufficient honesty; trade-up bridge |
| **Differentiate** | Decision engine for *your* shortlist — not a 15k discovery catalog |

**One-liner:** Lug²Lug helps you discover and log the box. Vitrine helps you decide the next move.

## Monetization rhyme

Their free collection + paid market depth supports Vitrine free scoring/wishlist + Pro alerts/comps — without flip-school positioning.
